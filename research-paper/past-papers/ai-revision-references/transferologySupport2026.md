# How to Use Will My Courses Transfer

## Source scope

This file preserves the official support content needed to evaluate the ACOSUS comparison table.
Screenshots, embedded videos, navigation, and unrelated footer content were omitted.

## Documented student workflow

The support article describes a student-facing workflow in which users enter courses, standardized examinations, and military credit.
Users identify the originating institution, academic term, department, and course before searching participating schools for transfer matches.

## Documented results

Transferology returns participating colleges and universities that have evaluated at least one entered item.
The results show how entered coursework or other credit has been evaluated for transfer or credit award.
Schools are ordered by match percentage, and users can filter the returned institutions.
The support page also distinguishes matches, possible matches, and items that do not currently match.

## Relevance to the ACOSUS paper

The official support workflow supports characterizing Transferology as a student-facing transfer-credit exploration tool.
It does not document ACOSUS-style transfer-specific survey collection, advisor profiles, or readiness measurement.
