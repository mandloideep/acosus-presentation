# ASSIST Resource Center

## Source scope

This file preserves the official description needed to evaluate the ACOSUS comparison table.
Navigation, images, login controls, and unrelated footer content were omitted.

## Documented purpose and institutional scope

ASSIST describes itself as California's official repository for articulation and transferability information.
Its stated scope includes California public colleges and universities as well as participating Association of Independent California Colleges and Universities institutions.

The student-facing description explains that prospective transfer students can use ASSIST to determine how California community college courses may satisfy elective, general-education, and major requirements at University of California campuses, California State University campuses, and participating independent institutions.

## Relevance to the ACOSUS paper

The official description supports characterizing ASSIST as an articulation and transferability information system with a California-specific institutional scope.
It does not describe the transfer-specific survey workflow, advisor profiles, readiness measure, or progressive modeling framework proposed by ACOSUS.
