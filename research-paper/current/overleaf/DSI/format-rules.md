# DSI Full Paper Submission — Formatting Rules

Distilled from `Initial-Full-Paper-Submission-TEMPLATE.docx`, `2023-DSI-Annual-Meeting_FINAL-Full-Paper-Submission-TEMPLATE.docx`, and `Full-Paper-Submission-Instructions.pdf`.

---

## Common rules (apply to BOTH Initial and Final submissions)

### Page setup
- Paper size: **US Letter** (8.5 × 11 in)
- Margins: **1 inch** on all four sides (top, bottom, left, right)
- **No** page numbers
- **No** headers, **no** footers, **no** running titles
- Single-sided layout

### Typography
- Font: **Arial**, 11 pt (sans-serif throughout the document)
- Line spacing: **single** (1.0 — explicitly not Word's default 1.15)
- No spacing before or after paragraphs
- Justification: **left** (ragged right)
- Do not use Word's pre-defined styles

### Top of page 1 (always present)
- First line: `DECISION SCIENCES INSTITUTE`, centered (kept verbatim, do not delete)
- Below it: paper title, centered
  - **Sentence case** — capitalize only the first letter of the title
  - **NOT bold**
  - Acronyms (e.g., AI, GPA, STEM) keep their normal capitalization

### Section structure
- Sections are separated by exactly **one blank line** (one hard return)
- Heading levels:
  - **Level 1** (e.g., `INTRODUCTION`, `LITERATURE REVIEW`): ALL CAPS, **bold**, left-aligned, no number
  - **Level 2** (e.g., `Literature review of topic 1`): **bold**, sentence case, no number
  - **Level 3** (e.g., `XY theory: key assumptions`): underlined, **not bold**, sentence case, no number

### Abstract
- Heading: the literal word `ABSTRACT`, **ALL CAPS**, **bold**, **centered**
- Body: ≤ **100 words**
- Single-spaced

### Keywords
- Line beginning `KEYWORDS:` (ALL CAPS, bold) followed by tab/space and a comma-separated list
- Use keywords from the DSJ or DSJIE list when possible

### Citations (in-text)
- **APA-style author-year**, parenthetical
- Examples:
  - Single author: `(Davenport, 2007)`
  - Two authors: `(Davenport & Harris, 2007)`
  - Three or more: `(Hopkins et al., 2010)`
  - Multiple sources: `(Davenport & Harris, 2007; Hopkins et al., 2010)`

### References list
- Heading: `REFERENCES` (ALL CAPS, bold, left-aligned)
- Sorted **alphabetically** by first author surname
- APA-style entries. Examples:
  - Davenport, T. H., & Harris, J. G. (2007). *Competing on analytics*. Boston: Harvard Business School Press, 46.
  - Liberatore, M. J., & Luo, W. (2010). The analytics movement: Implications for operations research. *Interfaces*, 40(4), 313–324.
  - Hopkins, M. S., LaValle, S., Balboni, F., Kruschwitz, N., & Shockley, R. (2010). 10 data points: Information and analytics at work. *MIT Sloan Management Review*, 52(1), 27–31.
- If references must be omitted (file size constraints), insert: `References available upon request.`

### Equations
- Each equation on its own line
- Numbered consecutively, with the number in parentheses **right-aligned**
- Example: `R1 = (X1) + (X2)                                     (1)`

### Footnotes
- **Do NOT use footnotes.**
- Where you would have added a footnote, use inline `[bracketed text]` instead.

### Tables and figures
- Place as close as possible to where they are first cited
- Total figure/graphic content should keep file size below 500 KB

### File and language
- Output: **PDF** (or non-`.docx` Word, or RTF). `.docx` is rejected.
- File size: ≤ **1 MB**
- Allowed character sets: traditional English or Greek. Avoid Korean, Chinese, etc.
- Page length norm: 6–35 pages inclusive of figures, tables, and references

### Copyright
- Material printed in full in the proceedings is considered published
- Authors retain copyright to all forms of presentation
- Do **not** submit content already copyrighted elsewhere

---

## Initial submission only (blind review)

- **NO author information anywhere** in the document — no names, no affiliations, no emails, no acknowledging footnotes that identify authors, no self-identifying phrases ("our prior work [Smith, 2020]")
- The title block consists of only:
  1. `DECISION SCIENCES INSTITUTE`
  2. Paper title
  3. (Then directly the `ABSTRACT` heading and body)
- Submit PDF for blind review

---

## Final submission only (after acceptance)

- Author block appears **on the second line below the title** (i.e., one blank line after the title, then author info)
- For each author, in order:
  - Full name (no titles like Dr., Professor, etc.)
  - Affiliation
  - Complete address
  - Email address
  - Telephone number
- All author lines: **single-spaced**, **centered**
- Multiple authors are separated by a blank line between author blocks
- Page-length norm: about 35 pages inclusive of everything
