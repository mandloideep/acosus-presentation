# Balanced advising-system comparison

Revision ID: `REV-COMP-001-ALT-B`.

This alternative combines transfer-specific tools with a broad student-success platform.

## Candidate systems

Retain ASSIST and Transferology/TES from the active comparison.

Add Navigate360 as a broad advising, coordinated-care, student-profile, survey, workflow, and early-alert platform.

Source: https://eab.com/solutions/navigate360/

## Strength of this alternative

This approach makes the advisor-dashboard comparison more direct because Navigate360 documents profiles, surveys, notes, alerts, appointment support, and staff workflows.

It also demonstrates that ACOSUS is not the only system that consolidates student context for advisors.

## Weakness of this alternative

Navigate360 is not a transfer-specific articulation system, and its public product page is a commercial source.

Adding it would widen the comparison table and require more explanation than the transfer-specific reviewer request requires.

The public page documents capabilities but does not permit independent comparison of performance, data quality, or fairness.

## Draft substitution

If selected, add a Navigate360 row describing its broad coordinated-care and workflow strengths.

Describe ACOSUS as narrower, transfer-specific, research-oriented, and less mature.

Do not claim that Navigate360 lacks transfer functionality unless an authoritative source explicitly establishes that absence.
