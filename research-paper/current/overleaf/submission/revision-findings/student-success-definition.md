# Student-success definition

Revision ID: `REV-SUCCESS-001`.

## Distal construct

In this paper, student success means continued enrollment or persistence after transfer, satisfactory academic progress and credit accumulation, and eventual completion of a bachelor’s degree.

These outcomes require institutional records and longitudinal follow-up.

The pilot did not observe them.

## Proximal operationalization

The Target Survey and priority-weighted readiness score provide a proximal readiness measure.

The measure summarizes self-reported academic preparation and confidence, commitment, time management, support and logistical context, institutional fit, prior experience, and career motivation.

It is appropriate for structured advising and for forming hypotheses about future outcomes.

It is not a validated probability of persistence or graduation.

## Terminology rules

Use `readiness score`, `survey-derived readiness`, or `readiness estimate` for current values.

Use `student success` only for the distal construct or when describing the broader research objective.

Use `prediction` only for the post-pilot KNN implementation or the proposed future research program.

Do not use `actual success rate` for a self-report or deterministic survey composite.
