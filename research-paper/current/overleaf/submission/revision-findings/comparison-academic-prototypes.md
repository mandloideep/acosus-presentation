# Academic-prototype comparison

Revision ID: `REV-COMP-001-ALT-C`.

This alternative compares ACOSUS with peer-reviewed or archived advising prototypes.

## Candidate systems

Mathieson and Lederer developed a transfer decision-support system that helped community-college students understand degree structures and identify courses that satisfy university requirements.

Source: https://aisel.aisnet.org/jise/vol7/iss3/3/

Nguyen, Doroudi, and Epstein evaluated a low-fidelity optimal-plan prototype against manual use of ASSIST and found fewer planning mistakes, faster completion, and higher usability for the prototype condition.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04500

Mattei and colleagues describe a decision-theoretic academic-advising tool and conclude that software can augment but should not replace the student-advisor relationship.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4113

SmartCourse integrates transcript and degree-plan context with a locally hosted language model and evaluates recommendation quality on representative advising queries.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22946

## Strength of this alternative

This comparison provides a stronger research-methodology discussion than commercial feature pages alone.

It also places ACOSUS within a longer history of decision support, course planning, and advisor augmentation.

## Weakness of this alternative

Only some prototypes focus directly on transfer students, and their evaluation tasks differ from the ACOSUS feasibility pilot.

Using this approach as the only comparison would not answer the reviewer’s request for existing operational transfer-advising systems as directly as the active approach.

## Draft substitution

If selected, compare problem scope, input data, user group, evaluation method, and evidence strength rather than attempting a product-feature checklist.

State that the ACOSUS pilot evaluated the survey workflow and student perceptions, whereas the other studies evaluated course selection, plan generation, stated preferences, or representative queries.
